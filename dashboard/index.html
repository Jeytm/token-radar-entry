How the implied value is built

implied = max(1, round(tvl_usd × 0.25 × growth × stability × maturity × category × chain × watchlist))

Base

25% of TVL, read as a launch valuation's starting multiple.

Growth

1 + clamp(7d change, −60, 120)/100 × 0.3: a hot week leans the multiple up, a collapse discounts it.

Stability

1.0 with no 7d figure, else clamp(1 − |change|/2, 0.7, 1.1): violent moves are discounted.

Maturity

0.6 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.4: three years of history earns the full 1.0; unknown age reads as 365 days.

Category

Curators 1.3, DEX and Lending 1.25, Perps 1.2, Derivatives and Launchpads 1.15, Oracle and Yield 1.1, Infra 1.05, CEX 0.5, everything else 1.0. CEX is discounted hard on purpose: an incumbent exchange launching a token is a corporate event, not an airdrop a radar can farm.

Chains

1.2 on three or more, 1.1 on two, 1.0 on one: multi-chain reach is a launch asset.

Watchlist

1.15 when the snapshot's watchlist names the protocol, else 1.0. The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling.

Who is scored

A protocol is pre-token when it records no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. Bridges, canonical bridges and liquid staking or restaking products are excluded: wrapped and staked products of tokens that trade are not tokens still to come.
